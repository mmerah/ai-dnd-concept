SCENARIO:
The Drowned Road
When Ashant's terraformers raised the sea, it swallowed the causeway out to Relay Nine, and the survey station was written off with its vault deck still sealed. Kael has come for what is locked down there: the founding survey nobody has read in thirty years. He starts at the siren mast on the Holdfast shore, where Ovid Sarn sounds the tide klaxon twice a day and nobody walks the drowned road without hearing it.
