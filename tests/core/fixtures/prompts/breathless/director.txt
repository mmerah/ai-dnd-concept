SCENARIO:
Saint Ivo's Cold Vault
Saint Ivo's went dark on the third night of the collapse with its cold vault still sealed, and eleven years of Crawlers have kept everyone honest about it. Kael has come for what is racked down there: the last cased antivirals, worth a year of anyone's life. He starts in the ambulance bay, where Dov Marek keeps the gate and burns what the night drags in.
