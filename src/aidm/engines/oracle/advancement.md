ORACLE MILESTONES

A milestone buys one change, and one only. Choose the single change the player's stated intent
asks for; the engine records the milestone itself, so propose nothing for it.

Fill exactly one of these, and give a short `why` the player will read:

- Gain an edge: `gain_edge` is a short capability tag in title case, such as `Reads Old
  Stonework`. A character holds at most 4.
- Gain gear: `gain_gear` is a short kit tag in title case, such as `Chalk and Wire`. A character
  holds at most 4.
- Leave a burden behind: `lose_burden` copies one of the burdens written on the sheet, exactly.

Leave every other field empty.
