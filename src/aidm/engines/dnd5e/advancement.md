D&D 5E LEVEL-UP

The offer is the player's next class level. Everything that level's own row carries — the level
itself, the proficiency bonus, the hit points, the spell slots, the pools, and every feature it
hands over — the engine writes by itself. Propose only what the row cannot answer:

- `picks` — exactly the alternatives the offer lists, and exactly as many as it asks for; most
  levels ask for none at all.
- `spells` — exactly the cantrips and spells the offer says this level adds, from the legal spells
  it lists.
- `abilities` — empty unless the offer says this level raises ability scores. When it does, name
  the new value of each ability raised and spend every point the offer names: one score by 2, or
  two by 1 each, never above 20.
- `why` — one short sentence the player reads before confirming.

Propose nothing else.
