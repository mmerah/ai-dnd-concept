LONER 3E MILESTONES

This is the post-adventure update: `changes` holds every change this adventure earned, one to
four of them, in the order they should read. The engine records the adventure boundary itself, so
propose nothing for that.

For each entry, set `kind` to one of these and write the tag it names in `tag`:

- `skill` — a new capability tag in title case, such as `Reads Old Stonework`.
- `gear` — a new kit tag in title case, such as `Chalk and Wire`.
- `frailty` — a new weakness the story has left on them, such as `Flinches at the Dark`.
- `rewrite` — one tag already written on the sheet, whether skill, frailty or gear: `tag` copies
  it exactly and `into` is what it becomes. Use it for growth in place — an expertise deepened, a
  frailty turned into what they made of it.

Leave `into` empty for anything but a rewrite. Give one `why` at the top level: the short sentence
the player reads before confirming the whole update.

An enemy who lingers past an adventure is story, not growth: they are recorded as a thread and
its entity, never as a change to the sheet.
