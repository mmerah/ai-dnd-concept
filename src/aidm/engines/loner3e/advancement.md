# LONER 3E MILESTONES

Return every change earned by this adventure, in reading order. Use 1-4 entries in `changes`. The engine records the finished adventure.

For each entry, set `kind` and `tag`:

- `skill`: a new capability in title case, such as `Reads Old Stonework`.
- `gear`: new signature gear in title case, such as `Chalk and Wire`.
- `frailty`: a new weakness, such as `Flinches at the Dark`.
- `rewrite`: growth of an existing skill, frailty, or gear tag. Copy its current text exactly into `tag` and put the new title-case text in `into`.

Leave `into` empty for the other kinds. Add one short `why` for the whole update.

Keep lasting enemies in the story as entities and threads, not sheet changes.
