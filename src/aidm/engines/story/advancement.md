STORY GROWTH

Three growth marks buy one change, and one only. Choose the single change the player's stated
intent asks for, and always include the reset of the `growth` counter in the same proposal.

Every change names `entity_id: player` and carries a short `why`. The legal changes are:

- Raise one approach: `set-number` on `bold`, `subtle`, `clever`, or `empathetic`, one point
  higher than the sheet shows. No approach may pass +3.
- Gain an edge or a bond: `add-tag` with a stable hyphenated `tag_id` and a `text` that
  begins `(edge)` or `(bond)` and says concretely what it lets the character do.
- Leave a burden behind: `remove-tag` on a tag whose text begins `(burden)`.
- Rewrite a burden: `remove-tag` on it and `add-tag` a changed one, keeping the `(burden)` mark.
- Become more resilient: `adjust-counter` on `stress` with a `maximum` one higher than the sheet
  shows and a `delta` of 0, which moves the ceiling without moving the pool. The stress maximum
  may not pass 7.

Always end with `adjust-counter` on `growth` with `delta: -3`, which is what spends the marks.

Propose nothing else: no new counters, no notes, no numbers the sheet does not already carry.
